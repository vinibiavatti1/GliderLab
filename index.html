Game of Life

An outer-totalistic cellular automaton played on a 2D grid, created by John Conway in 1970. Cells evolve through generations based on their 8 immediate neighbors using simple rules of birth, survival, and overpopulation, producing complex, emergent behaviors like gliders and oscillators.

Elementar Cellular Automata

An elementary cellular automaton is a 1D grid of binary cells that evolves over time based on its immediate neighbors. Using one of 256 simple rules, it can generate surprisingly complex, fractal, or chaotic patterns.
